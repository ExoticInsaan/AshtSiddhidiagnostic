About the centre

A neighbourhood lab built on careful testing.

Asht Siddhi Balaji Diagnostic Centre is a pathology laboratory on Hospital Road, Dausa. We run routine and advanced blood and urine tests and offer complete health-check packages at clear, honest prices.

Every sample is handled with care and processed on automated analysers, so the report you carry to your doctor is one you can rely on.

What we test

Tests that cover every check-up.

From a single blood count to a full-body profile, these are the tests our packages are built around.

Blood & Haematology

Complete blood count and ESR for a clear picture of your blood health.

Diabetes Care

Fasting glucose and HbA1c to track your sugar levels over time.

Liver & Kidney

Extended liver function, kidney function and eGFR in one visit.

Thyroid Profile

T3, T4 and TSH to check how your thyroid is working.

Heart & Lipids

Basic lipid profile to keep an eye on cholesterol and heart health.

Vitamins & Minerals

Vitamin D3, vitamin B12, calcium and iron profile.

Urine Examination

Routine urine tests that flag infections and kidney concerns early.

Joints & Inflammation

Quantitative CRP and RA factor for arthritis and inflammation.
